# Daily DSA plan

A 55-day path for learning DSA in JavaScript alongside Namaste DSA. It is also built into the app under **Daily plan**, where you can tick steps off.

## How each day works

1. **Warm up in the app** (about 10 min): read a concept, watch the visual, or solve an easy bridge problem.
2. **Namaste DSA lesson** (about 15–20 min): watch the lesson listed for the day.
3. **Solve on your own** (about 15 min): solve the matching problem in the app. Wait 5 minutes before opening a hint.
4. **Close** (2 min): without looking, write the key idea in one sentence.

**Try first** means you attempt the problem for 10–15 minutes *before* watching the lesson. Getting stuck is fine: it makes the lesson stick. **From memory** means a blank editor, no hints.

Every fifth day is a review day with no new lessons. If your recalls in the app are due, do them before anything else. Short on time? Do only the steps marked ★.

Study days are numbered, not dated. Missing a day never puts you "behind". Just do the next day.

Namaste DSA lessons are referenced by their public titles only. Practice problems in the app are original.
